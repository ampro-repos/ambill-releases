Features

Everything the day-to-day needs

The same modules your team already uses, organised so the work is quicker.

GST returns and reports

GSTR-1, GSTR-2 and GSTR-3B reports with export files, plus tax analysis and rate details.

e-Invoice and e-Way bill

Generate IRNs one by one or in batches, keep IRN history and print slips, and export e-way bill data.

Accounts you can rely on

Ledgers, vouchers, journal, day book, cash and bank book, trial balance, balance sheet and profit and loss.

Payments and banking

Receipt and payment vouchers, multiple payments in one go, cheque printing, deposit slips and bank reconciliation.

TDS and TCS

TDS processing, deduction reports and export forms, with settings for advance TDS.

APMC trading

Akada entry, awak lists, kharidi purchase bills and patti, nonda vikri, padtal and farmer transaction forecasting.

Inventory and production

Stock items and groups, bill of materials and production planning, with invoices, challans and builty prints.

Financial-year handling

Separate books per financial year, with balances carried forward and ledgers merged from other years.

Users and permissions

Role-based access so each person sees and does only what their job needs.

Acknowledgements (ACK)

Create, print, transfer and merge ACKs, including from another financial year, and show or hide them as needed.

Delivery challans

Create and print delivery challans, single or many at once, and track deposits and withdrawals.

Stock by chamber and floor

Chamber-wise and floor-wise stock reports, and balance bags by ACK, party, item and variety.

Rent tariffs and invoices

Set rent tariffs, raise rent invoices and follow rent-due reports month by month.

GST, e-Way bill and TDS

GSTR-1, 2 and 3B, e-way bill reports and TDS handling, the same way as in ambill.

Accounts and banking

Ledgers, vouchers, books, trial balance, balance sheet, profit and loss, payables, receivables, payment advice and cheque printing.

Requirements

What you need

Windows

Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or Windows 11. Windows Server 2016 and later also work.

Memory and disk

4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended) and about 3 GB free disk, more as your data grows.

Database

SQL Server Express is set up for you by the wizard if it is not already on the PC.

Display

1280 × 768 or larger. Full HD gives the most comfortable view.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which installer should I choose?

For daily work choose the financial-year installer for the year you are working in. Each year is its own app with its own database, and several years can sit side by side. The generic installer is a single app where you choose the year or years during setup.

Windows says the file isn’t commonly downloaded. Is it safe?

The installers are not code-signed yet, and Windows warns about new, unsigned files. Choose Keep in the browser, then Run anyway. We are working on signing the installers so this warning goes away.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only for the download and, on a PC without SQL Server, to fetch SQL Server Express during setup. If a PC will be offline, ask us for an offline installer.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions and offers to update. Each financial-year app updates only with its own year, so one year’s update never affects another.

Where is my data stored? What if I uninstall?

Your data lives in SQL Server on your own machine or server. Uninstalling the app does not delete your databases.

How do I activate my license?

ambill asks for your license when it first starts. Enter the details you were given, or contact your ampro representative.
